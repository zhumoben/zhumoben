# Notice

## Inkstone theme

The visual language of zhumoben — the paper/slate palette, the 4/6/10px radii,
the accent-tinted shadows, the serif headings with sans body, and the icon
weight — is adapted from **Inkstone**, a theme system designed and created by
**Zoro Chen** (https://github.com/wizcas).

The tokens here are a re-derivation for a darker, cooler surface (basalt /
inkstone rather than rice paper). They are not a copy of the upstream file; if
you want the original, see the theme guide in the asrun repository.
